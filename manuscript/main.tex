\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We study the real Clifford algebra $\Cl(3,3)\cong M_8(\R)$ of the split space $\R^{3,3}$, read as three temporal directions paired with
three spatial ones, and its graded tower $\Cl(3n,3n)$, and ask which ingredients of relativistic field theory the algebra fixes, which it
merely admits, and which it obstructs. At one constituent the joint commutant of the interior Lorentz algebra and the internal bivectors
fixes the imaginary unit up to sign, and the real Dirac theory built on it is ordinary complex Dirac theory. The same vector space carries
two readings, a displacement and a field strength; they share the metric and the Clifford action, but no equivariant map joins them. The
interior dynamics binds only in neutral signature, its force law closes in one way into a rotor, and its energy is not bounded below. At
three constituents an axial projector of rank $64$ carries one chiral Pati--Salam family with a right-handed neutrino; the electric charge
is identified among its compact generators, while the normal-plane charge is anomalous and cannot be the photon. The Lorentz-invariant
mass kernels of the block form a $272$-dimensional space, and an adopted potential selects the Standard-Model orbit under stated
conditions; global minimality is a conjecture supported by numerical searches. Curvature identities reproduce the Einstein tensor of a
given metric without a gravitational action. Every selection that goes beyond the algebra---the clock, the projector, the potential, the
couplings and the gauge principle that removes the surplus times---is an assumption, and the manuscript states where each enters.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} split-signature Clifford algebra; $\Cl(3,3)$; three times; Lorentz structures; complex structure;
Dirac theory; field-strength reading; chiral projectors; Pati--Salam; vacuum selection; curvature identities; causal structure;
obstructions; computer-verified mathematics.
\end{abstract}
