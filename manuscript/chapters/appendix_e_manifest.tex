\chapter{Verification manifest}
\label{app:manifest}

The executable checks of the statements of this manuscript are listed in \path{verification/MANIFEST.tsv}: for every check its
identifier, the script and command, the chapter and, where known, the manuscript label it supports, and whether it is exact or numerical.
A passing check shows that the coded assertion holds; it does not change the class of a statement. The theorem-to-code table generated from it, with the algorithms,
certificates and limitations of the checks, is \cref{app:repro}.
